How it works

Six commands, from empty profile to offer

You run it inside Claude Code on your own machine. Each step is a slash command you can run on its own.

01 · /setup

Build your profile

Reads your CV, LinkedIn export and past applications, or interviews you, and writes a structured profile.

02 · /scrape

Find roles

Searches job boards for postings that match your profile and removes duplicates.

03 · /rank

Shortlist the fits

Scores every new posting against your fit framework and returns a ranked shortlist with honest gaps.

04 · /apply

Tailor and send

Evaluates fit, drafts a CV and cover letter, has a second agent review them, then compiles clean PDFs.

05 · /interview

Prep each round

Builds a stage specific prep pack, maps likely questions to your STAR stories, and runs a mock interview.

06 · /outcome

Track results

Records each result, archives what you sent, and drafts polite follow ups when an application goes quiet.

Inside /apply

Why the applications come out sharp

  • Integrity gate first. Every posting is scanned for hidden text, invisible characters and instruction shaped lines before any agent reads it.
  • Drafter and reviewer. One agent writes. A second agent with a fresh context researches the company and critiques the draft before revision.
  • PDF checked, not just the source. It compiles the CV and cover letter and reads the rendered pages, fixing layout until the CV is exactly two pages and the letter one.
  • ATS text layer check. It extracts the CV's text the way an ATS parser sees it and confirms contact details, reading order and keyword coverage.
  • No fabrication. Claims are verified against your profile. Keyword gaps you cannot back up are left visible, never stuffed.

My notes

Use it well, use it safely

01

Feed it detail

A thin profile gives generic applications. Describe projects, tools and measurable results for every role you held.

02

Keep your copy private

Clone it into a private repository. A fork is public, and setup writes your contact details and salary expectations into the files.

03

Read before you send

Postings are treated as untrusted input, but skim every CV and letter before you hit submit, especially from unfamiliar job boards.

FAQ

Questions before you start

Is AI Job Search free?

The framework is free and MIT licensed. It runs inside Claude Code, which needs a paid Claude plan or Anthropic API credits, so budget for that.

Does it work for jobs in India?

Yes. The core workflow does not depend on country or language. Without a job board connector, the search step uses web search. You can also generate a connector for a board you use with the add portal command.

Will it invent skills or experience on my CV?

No. Every claim in the CV and cover letter is checked against the profile you build during setup. Skills you lack stay visible as gaps instead of being stuffed in.

Do I need to know how to code?

You need to be comfortable running a few terminal commands and installing Python, Bun and LaTeX. After that, you work with it in plain English through slash commands.

Is my data private?

It runs on your own machine. Keep your copy as a private repository, because a GitHub fork of a public repo is always public and setup writes your personal details into tracked files.

Who built it?

It is an independent open source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. I am not the author either. I am featuring it because it is the most complete agentic job search setup I have tried.
