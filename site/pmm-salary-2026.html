What moves the number

  • Company type beats city. The widest gaps in every source sit inside a single city, which points to company and level, not location.
  • Titles are inconsistent. A “Product Marketing Lead” at one company is a senior PMM at another. Compare scope, team size and revenue ownership, not the title.
  • The jump to Head is the biggest. Ranges roughly double between senior PMM and Head of Product Marketing.
  • Technical domains pay a premium. PMMs who can explain cloud security, AI or developer tools to buyers and sales are scarce.

Quick note on the data

This guide compiles publicly reported salaries. It is not my own survey. Self reported data skews to people who choose to share, sample sizes for senior roles are small, and the sources publish ranges rather than full distributions. Use it as a benchmark, then validate with recruiters and peers.
