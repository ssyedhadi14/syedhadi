FAQ

Before You Book

What is AEO?

Answer engine optimization. It makes your pages easy for ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to quote, so your brand shows up inside the answer.

Is AEO different from SEO?

It builds on SEO. Rankings still matter because AI answers pull from top results. AEO adds answer first content, schema, entity clarity and third party citations.

Can you guarantee a top three ranking?

No one honest can. I commit to the work that moves rankings and to monthly reporting on positions and AI share of voice.

Which CMS do you work with?

WordPress, Webflow, Framer, Netlify and most headless setups.
