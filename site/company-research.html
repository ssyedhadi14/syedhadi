    Common questions

    Company Research FAQ

    What does the company research tool find?

    Ten things a go to market team asks before a first call: market cap or latest valuation, headquarters, employee count, total customers, ARR or revenue, the top five competitors, unique differentiators, why customers choose the company over competitors, growth pain points, and the revenue opportunities the company is missing, each with a fix.

    Where does the data come from?

    A Firecrawl research agent starts on the homepage you enter, reads the about, customers, pricing and press pages, then searches the web: filings and investor relations, Crunchbase style profiles, LinkedIn, reputable press, review sites and job posts. Every figure carries a source link and a confidence label.

    How accurate are the numbers?

    Each figure is labelled HIGH when it comes from a filing or the company's own page, MEDIUM for reputable press or analyst estimates, and UNVERIFIED for a single weak source. Private companies rarely publish ARR or customer counts, so expect Not disclosed there. Never quote an unverified figure to a customer without a second source.

    Are the pain points and missed revenue facts?

    No. They are analysis. The agent ties each one to a visible signal such as a pricing page that forces every buyer to sales, no cloud marketplace listing, review complaints or a hiring pattern, and tells you the signal so you can judge it yourself.

    How long does it take?

    Usually one to four minutes, because the agent reads a dozen or more pages. Keep the tab open. Companies researched in the last seven days load instantly from cache, and the share link reopens the same report.

    Is there a limit?

    Yes. Each visitor can run three new companies an hour and the site has a daily cap, because every run costs research credits. Cached companies do not count. If you need a batch of accounts researched, book a call.

    Guide

    How Do You Research a Company Before a Sales Call?

    Quick answer

    Start with the company's own site for category, claims and logos, then check filings, funding news, headcount profiles, reviews and job posts. Note size, competitors, why buyers choose them, growth pain points and missed revenue. This tool does all of it from one homepage URL and returns a one page PDF.

    How to use it

    1. Paste the homepageAny public company or startup domain, such as accuknox.com.
    2. Let the agent researchIt reads the site and the open web for 1 to 4 minutes.
    3. Check confidence labelsHIGH, MEDIUM or UNVERIFIED on every figure, with its source.
    4. Download the one pagerShare it with sales, partners or leadership.

    Worked example

    A typical report covers market cap or valuation, HQ, employee count, customers, ARR, five competitors, four differentiators, buyer reasons, must fix and nice to fix pain points and three to five missed revenue plays with fixes.

    More questions people ask

    How do I find a private company's revenue?

    Private companies rarely publish revenue. Look for funding announcements, analyst estimates and press interviews, and treat any figure as an estimate. The tool marks these MEDIUM or UNVERIFIED.

    How do I find a company's competitors?

    Check comparison pages, review sites such as G2 and Gartner Peer Insights, analyst reports and the vendors buyers mention in reviews. The tool lists the top five with where they overlap.

    What is account research in B2B sales?

    Account research is gathering facts about a target company's size, priorities, buyers and pains before outreach, so the first message and call are specific to them.

    Is this company research tool free?

    Yes, with a limit of three new companies per visitor per hour. Companies researched in the last seven days load instantly from cache.
