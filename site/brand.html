Clear space

Keep space equal to the height of the S around every side of the lockup. Nothing else inside that box.

Minimum size

03 Typography

Four Fonts, Four Jobs

A geometric display face for headlines, an italic serif for a single accent word, a neutral body face and a mono for labels. The contrast between the sans and the serif is the whole personality.

Pipeline is the only scoreboard.
DisplayPlus Jakarta SansWeights 600, 700, 800

Headlines, numbers, card titles, the wordmark. Tight tracking, minus 3.5% on display sizes.

growth, in public
AccentInstrument SerifWeights 400 italic

One word per headline, pull quotes, the tagline. Never for body copy, never more than a phrase.

Six years of B2B SaaS go to market taught me one thing: the useful stuff never makes it into a deck.
BodyInterWeights 400, 500, 600

Paragraphs, UI, forms, tables. 16px on web, 1.6 line height.

GTM / PMM / PARTNERS / AEO
LabelsJetBrains MonoWeights 500

Eyebrows, dates, tags, chart ticks. Uppercase with 0.2em tracking at 12px.

Headline formula

Sentence case, Plus Jakarta Sans 800, minus 3.5% tracking, line height 1.02. Exactly one word or phrase in Instrument Serif italic, coloured accent.

Scale

Display 56 to 88px. Section titles 34 to 56px. Card titles 18 to 22px. Body 16px, small 14px, labels 12px uppercase.

05 Voice

How It Sounds

If you are writing copy that carries the name, these five rules keep it consistent with everything on this site.

Numbers before adjectives

$2M of pipeline on a $25K quarter says more than "high impact". If there is a number, lead with it.

Active voice, short sentences

"Partners sourced 40% of pipeline", not "40% of pipeline was sourced through partner channels".

Plain punctuation

No em dashes, no semicolons, no exclamation marks. Commas and full stops carry the rhythm.

Prevention first, GTM always

The subject is pipeline and how a technical product gets bought. Security is the industry, not the tone.

Name, then role

First mention: Syed Hadi, Product Marketing and Partnerships Lead at AccuKnox. After that, Syed.

Links that earn a click

Point to syedcreates.com, the calculators or Pipeline Notes. Never to a generic bio page.
