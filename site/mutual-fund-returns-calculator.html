FAQ

Common Questions

What is CAGR?

CAGR, or compound annual growth rate, is the average yearly return that turns the starting NAV into today's NAV. A 12% CAGR over 10 years means money roughly tripled.

Why do some funds show no 15 or 20 year return?

The fund, or its data in the public AMFI record, is younger than that period. Direct plans only started in January 2013, so returns longer than that use the regular plan of the same fund, marked with an R.

Direct plan or regular plan?

A direct plan has no distributor commission, so its expense ratio is lower and returns are usually higher than the regular plan of the same fund. You buy direct plans on the AMC website or on direct investing platforms.

Will a fund repeat its past returns?

Not necessarily. Past returns show how a fund behaved, not what it will do. The calculator lets you use a cautious return so you can see a safer case.

How are mutual fund gains taxed?

Equity fund gains on units held over 12 months are taxed at 12.5% above Rs 1.25 lakh a year. Units held 12 months or less are taxed at 20%. Debt fund units bought after 1 April 2023 are taxed at your slab rate.

Is this a recommendation to buy these funds?

No. The list covers large, well known funds across categories for education. It is not a ranking or a recommendation. Syed Hadi is not a SEBI registered investment adviser.
