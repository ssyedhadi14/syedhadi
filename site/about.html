Product marketing and partnerships lead in Bengaluru, building GTM for cloud and AI security since 2021.

Hi there

I’m Syed Hadi.

I lead product marketing and channel partnerships at AccuKnox, a Zero Trust CNAPP and AI security company. I joined in 2021 when the product was still being built and have worked across project management, product, marketing, sales and partnerships since.

I work best with security and SaaS teams past the zero to one stage who need a sharp story, a partner motion and an inbound engine that compounds into ARR. I completed an MBA at BITS Pilani alongside the day job.

How I work

Human Built Strategy, Powered by AI

Four steps I run on every launch, campaign and partner program.

01

Research and positioning

Talk to buyers, partners and sales. Map the competitive field. Land one sharp story the whole company can repeat.

02

Build the system

Turn the story into pages, decks, content and the apps and agents that keep the engine running without manual work.

03

Launch and enable

Ship it through webinars, events, partners and AI search, then train sales and partners until they sell it better than I do.

04

Measure and compound

Tie every motion to influenced pipeline and ARR. Keep what compounds, cut what does not.
