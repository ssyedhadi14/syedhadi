How it works

The Rules Behind the Numbers

BMI. Weight in kg divided by height in metres squared. Scored on the Asian Indian bands: healthy 18.5 to 22.9, overweight 23 to 24.9, obesity 25 and above. The WHO global category is shown alongside.

Belly fat. A waist of 90 cm or more for men, 80 cm or more for women, or a waist to height ratio above 0.5. The 2025 revised Indian definition of obesity treats BMI above 23 with excess belly fat as the more serious stage.

Readings. Blood pressure on ACC/AHA bands, fasting glucose and HbA1c on ADA cut offs, SGPT on gastroenterology healthy limits (33 U/L men, 25 U/L women).

The plan. Each start or stop row is triggered by your numbers: activity under 150 minutes, steps under 7,000, sleep under 7 hours, sitting 6 hours or more, sugary drinks, or a flagged reading. Activity targets follow WHO guidance.

Not medical advice. This tool is for education and habit building. It does not diagnose anything. Talk to a doctor about any flagged reading, and get care the same day for blood pressure of 180 / 120 or more.
FAQ

Common Questions

What is a healthy BMI for Indians?

For Asian Indian adults the healthy range is 18.5 to 22.9. A BMI of 23 to 24.9 counts as overweight and 25 or above as obesity. These cut offs are lower than the WHO global ones (25 and 30) because Indians develop diabetes and heart disease at lower body weight, which is why Indian doctors use them.

How do I calculate BMI?

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres squared. At 170 cm and 72 kg that is 72 ÷ (1.7 × 1.7) = 24.9. The calculator does this for you and shows the weight range that is healthy for your height.

Why does waist size matter as much as BMI?

Fat around the belly drives insulin resistance, fatty liver and heart risk, and Indians carry more of it at the same BMI. The 2025 revised Indian definition of obesity uses BMI above 23 together with a waist of 90 cm or more for men, 80 cm or more for women, or a waist to height ratio above 0.5.

What blood pressure, sugar and SGPT readings are normal?

Blood pressure under 120 / 80 is normal and 130 / 80 or more is high. Fasting glucose under 100 mg/dL and HbA1c under 5.7% are normal, with 100 to 125 and 5.7 to 6.4% in the prediabetes range. For SGPT (ALT), gastroenterology guidance puts the healthy upper limit near 33 U/L for men and 25 U/L for women, though many labs print 40. Your doctor reads these with your full history.

How much weight should I aim to lose first?

Five to seven percent of your current weight over six months. For a 78 kg adult that is about 4 to 5.5 kg. Losses of this size measurably lower blood pressure, blood sugar and liver fat, even before you reach the healthy BMI range.

Is my data saved or shared?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Health readings never go into the share link. Habit ticks are saved only on this device and reset every Monday. The PDF is built on your device.

Guide

How Do You Calculate BMI for Indians?

Quick answer

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. For Asian Indian adults the healthy range is 18.5 to 22.9, 23 to 24.9 is overweight and 25 or more is obesity. These cut offs are lower than the global WHO scale because health risk starts at a lower BMI in Indians.

How to use it

  1. Enter height and weightHeight in centimetres and weight in kilograms, measured in the morning.
  2. Add your waistMeasure at the navel. Waist size flags belly fat that BMI can miss.
  3. Fill your daily habitsSteps, cardio, sleep, sitting time and food. These drive the start and stop table.
  4. Get your planRead the insights, track 8 habits for 7 days and download the PDF plan.

The formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²
Waist to height ratio = waist (cm) ÷ height (cm)

Worked example

170 cm and 78 kg: 78 ÷ 1.70² = 27.0. On Indian cut offs that is in the obesity band, while the WHO global scale calls it overweight. The healthy weight for 170 cm on Indian cut offs is about 53 to 66 kg.

More questions people ask

What is a normal BMI for Indian adults?

18.5 to 22.9 on the Asian Indian cut offs used by Indian guidelines. The global WHO scale uses 18.5 to 24.9.

Why is the BMI cut off lower for Indians?

At the same BMI, Indians tend to carry more body fat and belly fat, which raises the risk of diabetes and heart disease earlier. Lower cut offs catch that risk sooner.

Is waist size more important than BMI?

Both matter. A waist of 90 cm or more for men, or 80 cm or more for women, or a waist to height ratio above 0.5, signals belly fat even when BMI looks normal.

How many steps a day should I walk?

Around 7,000 to 10,000 steps a day is linked with lower health risk in large studies. Start from your current count and add about 1,000 a week.
